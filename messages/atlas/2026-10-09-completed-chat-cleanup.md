## Selection and activity protection

Age begins at successful native turn completion with a final response. A completed status-check chat does not establish that the underlying nightly business workflow has finished. Selection is restricted to the exact local allowlisted five-minute automation identities and untouched successful original turns. The reviewed helper excludes active, failed or interrupted native turns, manually resumed chats, queued input, pinned or organized chats, incomplete goals and chats with spawned descendants. Native successful completion means a completed turn with no native error and a final; the helper does not classify business failures described in that final. Existing notification claims and delivery-confirmation rules remain unchanged.

The helper rechecks eligibility and invokes the exact native CLI through `--no-daemon archive`. The native writer lock refuses an active writer; a metadata read followed by shared-daemon archival is not an equivalent guard. The native fixture proof covers idle success, locked root rejection, locked transitive descendant rejection with unchanged original bytes, and success after releasing locks, including legacy and paginated metadata. These are offline storage tests, not model runs. Exact binary drift or unsupported native schema blocks cleanup.

Durable local receipts preserve attempted and uncertain outcomes and prevent blind re-archival after a user restores a chat. Native archival remains recoverable; the helper does not delete transcripts or directly write native databases.
